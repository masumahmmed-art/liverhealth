Exercise for Liver Health

Even without weight loss, exercise directly reduces liver fat. Aim for a mix of these three types.

150 minof moderate activity per week — the widely cited general target
No weight loss requiredexercise reduces liver fat even before the scale moves
2–3x / weekresistance training frequency linked to better insulin sensitivity

Aerobic

Brisk walking, cycling, or swimming for 30 min, 5 days a week — the most consistently effective type for burning liver fat.

Resistance

Bodyweight or weights 2–3x weekly. Builds muscle mass, which improves insulin sensitivity and liver metabolism.

HIIT

Short bursts of intense activity — 15–20 min sessions, 2x weekly, are linked to meaningful drops in liver fat markers.

Start with what you can manage. A 10-minute walk after meals beats nothing. Consistency beats intensity — and check with your doctor before starting a new routine if you have an existing health condition.

About This Site
LiverHealthHub is an independent educational project — it isn't a medical practice, clinic, or affiliated with any hospital or health system. It's maintained by one person, motivated by how common and how preventable most liver disease actually is, with the goal of turning scattered medical research into plain-language guidance people can actually use.

The content is researched and written by summarizing peer-reviewed studies and guidance from major health authorities (see the Research page for dated, sourced summaries and the citation behind the Liver Fat Estimate calculator) — it is not written or reviewed by a licensed medical professional, and nothing here should be treated as personalized medical advice.

Built with reference to how major health publishers (Mayo Clinic, Healthline, NHS, Liver UK) structure liver-health content: symptom guides, risk breakdowns, and dated, sourced research summaries.
