Milk Thistle (Silymarin)

From $19.95

The most studied herbal supplement for liver support, often standardised to 80% silymarin, used for antioxidant and anti-inflammatory support.

Available in: 🇦🇺 Australia · 🇺🇸 United States · 🇪🇺 European Union

Key points:
  • Standardised extract: 140–420 mg silymarin daily
  • Best evidence for Amanita mushroom poisoning and toxin-related liver injury
  • Mixed evidence for MASLD; may modestly improve liver enzymes
  • Generally well tolerated; rare mild digestive upset
  • May interact with medications metabolised by CYP450 enzymes

N-Acetyl Cysteine (NAC)

From $24.50

A precursor to glutathione, the liver's master antioxidant. NAC supports the body's natural detoxification pathways.

Available in: 🇦🇺 Australia · 🇺🇸 United States · 🇪🇺 European Union

Key points:
  • Common dose: 600–1,200 mg daily (medical supervision for higher doses)
  • Primary clinical use: paracetamol overdose antidote in hospitals
  • May support glutathione levels under oxidative stress
  • Some evidence for insulin resistance in PCOS/metabolic syndrome
  • Can cause nausea at higher doses — take with food

Vitamin E (Mixed Tocopherols)

From $16.90

High-quality vitamin E is sometimes used for MASLD/MASH under medical supervision.

Available in: 🇦🇺 Australia · 🇺🇸 United States · 🇪🇺 European Union

Key points:
  • Studied dose for NASH: 800 IU/day of mixed tocopherols
  • Shown in some trials to reduce liver enzymes and steatosis
  • Not recommended for people with diabetes or heart disease without medical oversight
  • High doses may raise bleeding risk — stop before surgery
  • Look for "mixed tocopherols" rather than synthetic dl-alpha-tocopherol

Omega-3 Fish Oil

From $22.00

High-EPA/DHA omega-3s may help reduce liver fat and inflammation markers in people with MASLD.

Available in: 🇦🇺 Australia · 🇺🇸 United States · 🇪🇺 European Union

Key points:
  • Effective dose: 2–4 g combined EPA+DHA daily
  • Associated with reduced liver fat and improved liver enzymes in meta-analyses
  • Also benefits triglycerides and cardiovascular health
  • Choose molecularly distilled products to avoid contaminants
  • May increase bleeding risk at high doses — caution with anticoagulants

About This Site
LiverHealthHub is an independent educational project — it isn't a medical practice, clinic, or affiliated with any hospital or health system. It's maintained by one person, motivated by how common and how preventable most liver disease actually is, with the goal of turning scattered medical research into plain-language guidance people can actually use.
